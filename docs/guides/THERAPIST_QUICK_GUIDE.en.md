# FamilyBridge therapist quick guide

中文版: [THERAPIST_QUICK_GUIDE.zh-CN.md](THERAPIST_QUICK_GUIDE.zh-CN.md).

FamilyBridge supports your DBT-A group between sessions. **It is not monitored in real time.**
Safety events wait in your queue until you open it; nobody is contacted automatically. Tell
families this in the first session, and point them to the Help screen and the hospital's
contacts.

You see only your own groups. Adolescent and caregiver data stay separate: a caregiver sees an
adolescent's diary items only when the adolescent shares them.

## Finding your way

After you sign in you land on the **Portal**: your account details, **Settings**, and a card for
each area. **Family Bridge** holds Groups & families, Schedule, Diary review and Joint sessions.
**Personal Learning** holds Progress and Content review (a family's Learning review opens from
the family page). The **Safety queue** and **Metrics** stay in the sidebar wherever you are.

## Getting started

1. Redeem the code from the admin at `/redeem`. Choose a username, a display name, your language
   and a password.
2. **Groups & families:** create a group, then a family in it.
3. In the family, issue one **teen** code and one or more **caregiver** codes. Each code is shown
   once: copy it and give it to the person directly. Each person redeems their own code at
   `/redeem` and goes through consent on their own.
4. Forgotten password: open the family and issue a **reset code** for that person.

## Weekly routine

| Screen | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Safety queue** | Open first. Acknowledge new events, review, add notes, and resolve with a follow-up. You can also record an event by hand. |
| **Schedule** | Set how many diary days per week you expect, and the homework for each role. Participants see "Done" or "Not yet"; nothing is shown as overdue. Record which skills each group session covered: adolescents then get a Group review lesson. |
| **Diary review** | Choose a family. Each person has a separate panel with trends, skill use and entries. Opening it is audited. |
| **Progress** | A coverage map of skills practised per person. There are no totals or scores. |
| **Learning review** (from the family page) | Lesson summaries, check-in status and moods. A participant's own words open only when you ask, and that read is audited. |
| **Content review** | Mark cached AI explanations and quizzes "reviewed" or "needs change". "Needs change" withdraws the item. |
| **Metrics** | Enrolment, diary completion against your expectation, homework, learning, safety acknowledgement time. Enter recruitment numbers (screened, eligible, approached, consented) per period here. |

## Joint sessions

1. On the family page, mark the parent–adolescent pair **suitable** when you judge it right.
2. On **Joint sessions**, invite the pair to an episode in a time window (up to 14 days).
3. Each person answers on their own. If either says no, the other sees only "This session won't
   run this time"; no reason is asked or shown.
4. During the session the board shows **step progress only**, not what they write.
   - **Pause** pauses for both; either person can carry on.
   - **I need to stop** holds the room: the person who stopped sees Help, the partner sees
     "paused for now". Only you can **Resume** or **End** it. Check in with the family first.
5. Afterwards, the summary shows check-in ratings and private reflections. Opening it is audited.
   Tell families before the session that you will see their reflections.

## Safety events

- Events come from: a high-risk phrase in a lesson message (the lesson stops and Help opens), a
  diary urge at or above the site's threshold, a worrying diary note, "I need to stop" in a joint
  session, and events you record yourself.
- The participant sees Help, which lists the hospital's contacts and staffed hours. They are
  **not** told that anyone has been alerted.
- Follow your hospital's risk protocol. FamilyBridge does not replace it.

## What participants never see

Scores, streaks, badges, rankings, "failed" or overdue messages, or each other's private entries.
There is no messaging inside the app.
