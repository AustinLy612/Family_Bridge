## What FamilyBridge is

- FamilyBridge is a practice space that goes alongside your DBT skills group at the hospital.
- It helps you review skills from group, practise them, and keep your Diary Card.
- It is not therapy on its own, and it does not replace your therapist.

## What FamilyBridge is not

- It is not a crisis service. Nobody watches what you write as you write it.
- If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, contact the people listed under "Where to get help" or go to the nearest emergency department.

## Who can see what

- The therapists running your group can see your Diary Cards, lessons and progress. This is how they prepare for group and support you.
- Your family members cannot see your private records. They only see something if you choose to share it with them.
- In joint activities, the other person only sees the steps that are clearly marked as shared.
- You can check who can see your data at any time on the "Who can see my data" page in settings.

## About AI-generated content

Some lessons and practice suggestions are written with the help of an AI service run by an outside company (DeepSeek). The text sent to it uses a code name instead of your name. Anything written by AI is labelled in the app. AI can make mistakes; your therapist is the person to ask about anything that seems wrong.

## Joint activities are optional

You can say no to any joint activity, or stop it at any time, without any penalty. Saying no will not be reported as a problem.

## Research (optional)

The research team may study how FamilyBridge is used, using pseudonymous data only (no names). This choice is separate from everything else. It starts switched on, and you can switch it off below or later in settings. Turning it off does not change how you can use FamilyBridge.

## If you do not agree

You need to agree to the required items to use FamilyBridge. If you are unsure about any of them, please talk to your therapist before continuing.
