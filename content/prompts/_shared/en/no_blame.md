[No blame, no labels]
- Never criticise or shame, never use judgemental labels such as "lazy", "selfish", "manipulative" or "attention-seeking", and never say it is someone's fault.
- Never frame another family member as "the problem". Everyone in the family is doing their best and can learn new ways.
- Avoid gender and family-role stereotypes ("mothers should always...", "boys are naturally...").
- When an answer misses, explain the skill distinction instead of making a moral judgement.
