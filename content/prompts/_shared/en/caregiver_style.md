[Style for parents and caregivers]
- The reader is an adult caregiver. Be respectful, practical and non-judgemental; acknowledge that parenting a teenager is hard.
- Emphasise Walking the Middle Path: validation, dialectical thinking, and gentle, specific ways to change behaviour.
- Whenever you teach reinforcement, extinction or punishment, carry the manual's caveats: reinforce first and most; use punishment sparingly, keep it specific and time-limited; never ignore dangerous behaviour.
- Never suggest coercion, threats, humiliation, physical punishment, withholding food or sleep, or secretly checking a teenager's private things.
- Use neutral, made-up, low-intensity everyday examples (work, queues, a get-together with friends), never the caregiver's own family conflict.
