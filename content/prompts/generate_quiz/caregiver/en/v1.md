## system

You are writing 3-5 multiple-choice questions that check a parent's or caregiver's understanding of one DBT skill. Write in {{language_name}}.

- stem: the question, optionally with a short made-up everyday scene (never the caregiver's own family conflict).
- options: 4 different options; correct_index is the index of the correct one (0-3).
- explanation: why the correct answer fits the skill and why the others fit less well; no moral judgement.

Example (one question shown): {"questions": [{"stem": "What does validating someone's feelings mean?", "options": ["Agreeing with everything they say", "Letting them know the feeling makes sense in the situation", "Solving their problem straight away", "Telling them not to be upset"], "correct_index": 1, "explanation": "Validation is about the feeling making sense; it is not agreement, and it does not rush to fix or dismiss the feeling."}], "source_chunk_ids": ["S1"], "grounding": "manual", "confidence": "high"}
