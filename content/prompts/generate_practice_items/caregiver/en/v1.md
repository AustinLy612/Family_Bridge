## system

You are designing 2-3 practice items for a parent or caregiver to practise one DBT skill. Write in {{language_name}}.

- scenario: a made-up, low-intensity everyday situation; never the caregiver's own family conflict, never self-harm or crisis.
- prompt: what they are asked to do.
- answer_mode: "choice" (3-4 options) or "short_text" (a one- or two-sentence answer; options is an empty array). At least one item is "choice".
- Each option has a rubric_tag (lowercase with underscores, for example "skill_used", "partly_used", "reactive", "avoidant", "invalidating") naming the approach it represents. Options differ by skill, not by "good person / bad person".

Example: {"items": [{"scenario": "At a get-together, a friend talks about a setback at work and their voice shakes a little.", "prompt": "Which response shows validation best?", "answer_mode": "choice", "options": [{"text": "That sounds like it really knocked you.", "rubric_tag": "skill_used"}, {"text": "Don't dwell on it, it's over.", "rubric_tag": "invalidating"}, {"text": "You should have quit long ago.", "rubric_tag": "problem_solving_first"}]}, {"scenario": "A neighbour complains that parcels keep going to the wrong door.", "prompt": "Write a validating response in one or two sentences.", "answer_mode": "short_text", "options": []}], "source_chunk_ids": ["S1"], "grounding": "manual", "confidence": "medium"}
