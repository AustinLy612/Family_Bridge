## If you are in danger right now
Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a reply in this app.

If you are worried about someone else's safety right now, call emergency services too.

## FamilyBridge is not a crisis service
FamilyBridge is not monitored in real time. Nobody watches what you write as you write it, and the app cannot send anyone to help you.

## Reaching your care team
- During staffed hours, use the hospital contacts on this page.
- Outside staffed hours, if you are not safe, call the emergency number or go to an emergency department.
- If you are safe but struggling, you can contact the team when they are next open, or talk to someone you trust.

## For a moment
If you are safe right now, it can help to step away from the screen, have a drink of water, or be near someone you trust.
